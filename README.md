## 💡 Project Overview

The **Lease Management System** is a Salesforce-based cloud application designed to automate and streamline the processes involved in property leasing.  
It enables property owners and managers to efficiently handle **lease agreements, tenant details, payments, and approval workflows** — reducing manual effort and ensuring better accuracy and tracking.

---

## ⚙️ Key Features

- 🏢 **Custom Objects:** Property, Tenant, Lease, and Payment  
- 🔄 **Automation:** Apex Triggers, Record-Triggered Flows, and Scheduled Apex Classes  
- 📧 **Communication:** Email Templates for Payment Reminders, Lease Approvals, and Notifications  
- ✅ **Validation Rules:** Ensures data integrity and prevents invalid entries  
- 🧾 **Approval Process:** Automates leave requests and lease renewals  
- 📆 **Scheduled Automation:** Monthly payment reminders sent automatically  
- 🧪 **Testing & Debugging:** Verified through multiple test scenarios ensuring stability  
- 📊 **Documentation:** Includes all development and testing records
